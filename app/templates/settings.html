Privacy and Legal

BrainDump processes personal and study-related data only to provide and improve core platform features, including task management, study planning, flashcards, summarization, Pomodoro tracking, and calendar support.

Data We Store

  • Account and authentication information required for sign-in and account management.
  • Study content you create, including tasks, flashcards, notes, summaries, and study plans.
  • Activity records needed to deliver progress features, such as Pomodoro session history.

How Data Is Used

  • To operate core BrainDump functionality and maintain your personalized workspace.
  • To process content you submit for AI-enabled features such as planning and summarization.
  • To support calendar workflows when you explicitly enable scheduling integrations.

Integrations

  • Google Calendar access is used exclusively for synchronization and scheduling functions.
  • Integration access is optional and can be revoked by disconnecting the linked account.
  • Third-party services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Legal

  • BrainDump is provided for educational productivity and academic support purposes.
  • Users remain responsible for reviewing and validating generated content before academic use.
  • By using the platform, you agree to lawful, ethical, and responsible use of the service.
